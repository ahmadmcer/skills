# SBL Jewish Citations and Standard Abbreviations

A comprehensive reference for formatting Jewish scriptural, rabbinic, and legal citations according to the **SBL Handbook of Style (2nd ed. §8.3.8)** and standard academic norms.

---

## 1. SBL Prefixes for Rabbinic Strata

To avoid ambiguity between different rabbinic collections that share identical tractate names, the SBL Handbook of Style requires the following lowercase italicized prefixes:

| Prefix | Corpus | Example Citation | Full Source Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`m.`** | Mishnah | `m. Ber. 1:1` | Mishnah Berakhot, chapter 1, mishnah 1 |
| **`t.`** | Tosefta | `t. Ber. 1:1` | Tosefta Berakhot, chapter 1, halakhah 1 |
| **`b.`** | Talmud Bavli | `b. Šabb. 31a` | Babylonian Talmud Shabbat, folio 31, side a |
| **`y.`** | Talmud Yerushalmi | `y. Ber. 1:1` | Jerusalem Talmud Berakhot, chapter 1, halakhah 1 |

> [!NOTE]
> Titles of ancient primary sources (such as Mishnah, Talmud, Berakhot, Shabbat) are **not italicized** in plain text, but the prefix abbreviation is italicized in formal academic print (e.g. *b. Ber. 2a*). In markdown output, plain ASCII prefixes (`b. Ber. 2a` or `b. Shabbat 31a`) are widely accepted.

## 4. Major Halakhic Codes Citation Syntax

### 1. Rambam's Mishneh Torah (Yad HaChazakah)
Cited by title, section (halakhic tractate), chapter, and halakhah:
- Format: `Mishneh Torah, Hilkhot [Section] [Chapter]:[Halakhah]`
- Examples:
  - `Mishneh Torah, Hilkhot Shabbat 1:1`
  - `Mishneh Torah, Hilkhot De'ot 2:3`
  - `Mishneh Torah, Hilkhot Teshuvah 3:4`

### 2. The Shulchan Aruch (Rabbi Yosef Karo & Rema)
Organized into 4 major divisions (*Arba'ah Turim*). Cited by Division, *Siman* (chapter), and *Se'if* (paragraph):
- **Orach Chayim (OC / או״ח)**: Daily life, prayer, tzitzit, tefillin, Shabbat, festivals.
  - Example: `Shulchan Aruch, Orach Chayim 1:1` (waking up like a lion to serve the Creator).
- **Yoreh De'ah (YD / יו״ד)**: Dietary laws (kashrut), vows, usury, nidda, mourning, conversion.
  - Example: `Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh De'ah 87:1` (mixing meat and milk).
- **Even HaEzer (EH / אה״ע)**: Marriage, divorce, procreation, marital relationships.
  - Example: `Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer 1:1`.
- **Choshen Mishpat (CM / חו״מ)**: Civil law, damages, contracts, judges, loans, property.
  - Example: `Shulchan Aruch, Choshen Mishpat 378:1`.

## 5. Punctuation & Transliteration Rules

1. **Parenthetical Format**: Tanakh citations belong inside parentheses: `(Gen 1:1 NJPS)` or `(Tehillim 23:1)`.
2. **En-Dashes (`–`)**: Range numbers must use an en-dash (`–`), never a hyphen (`-`): `(Deut 6:4–9)`, `b. Šabb. 31a–b`.
3. **Colons & Semicolons**:
   - Chapter and verse separated by a colon: `Exod 20:2`.
   - Multiple verses in same chapter: `Exod 20:2, 7, 12`.
   - Multiple chapters separated by semicolon: `Gen 1:1; 12:1; Exod 3:14`.
4. **Hebrew Consonant Transliteration**:
   - `ח` (*Chet*): Transliterated as `ch` or `ḥ` (e.g. *Chukat* or *Ḥuqqat*).
   - `כ` / `ך` (*Khaf*): Transliterated as `kh` or `ḵ`.
   - `צ` (*Tsadi*): Transliterated as `tz`, `ts`, or `ṣ`.
   - `שׂ` (*Sin*): Transliterated as `s` or `ś`.
   - `שׁ` (*Shin*): Transliterated as `sh` or `š`.
