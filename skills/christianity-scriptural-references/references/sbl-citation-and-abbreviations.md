# SBL Citation & Abbreviations Guide

Read this guide when formatting scripture references according to the **Society of Biblical Literature
(SBL) Handbook of Style** (2nd Edition), utilizing standard book abbreviations, and formatting citations.

---

## 1. SBL In-Text & Parenthetical Citation Rules

1. **Parenthetical Format**:
   - Place biblical citations in parentheses: `(John 3:16)` or include the version: `(John 3:16 ESV)`.
   - **No comma** between verse and version: `(Rom 8:28 NIV)` — **not** `(Rom 8:28, NIV)`.
2. **Punctuation Rules**:
   - Colon between chapter and verse: `Matt 28:19`.
   - En-dash (`–`) for verse ranges: `Gen 1:1–3`.
   - Comma between non-consecutive verses: `Ps 23:1, 4`.
   - Semicolon between distinct chapters or books: `Luke 1:5; 2:1` or `1 Cor 13:4–8; Gal 5:22–23`.
3. **Beginning of Sentence**:
   - If a sentence begins with a book name, spell it out completely: _"First Corinthians 13 describes love."_ — **never** start a sentence with an abbreviation like _"1 Cor 13 describes love."_
