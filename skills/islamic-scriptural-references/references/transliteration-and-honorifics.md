# Arabic Transliteration & Islamic Honorifics Guide

Read this guide when rendering Arabic Islamic terminology into the Latin alphabet using the
ALA-LC standard, applying diacritics, and utilizing respectful honorifics.

---

## 1. ALA-LC Romanization System

The American Library Association & Library of Congress (ALA-LC) system is the academic standard
for unambiguous transliteration of Arabic into Latin script.

### Vowels & Diacritics
- **Short Vowels**: *Fatḥah* &rarr; **a**, *Kasrah* &rarr; **i**, *Ḍammah* &rarr; **u**.
- **Long Vowels**:
  - Alif (ا) / Alif Maqṣūrah (ى) &rarr; **ā** (e.g. *Qur’ān*, *ṣalāh*, *Zakāh*)
  - Yā’ (ي) &rarr; **ī** (e.g. *ḥadīth*, *tafsīr*)
  - Wāw (و) &rarr; **ū** (e.g. *wuḍū’*, *ṣūm*)
- **Diphthongs**: **aw** (e.g. *tawḥīd*), **ay** (e.g. *ghayb*).
- **Tā’ Marbūṭah (ة)**: Transliterated as **h** in isolation (e.g. *Sunnah*), and as **t** in a genitive construct (*Iḍāfah*, e.g. *Sunnat al-Nabī*).
- **Definite Article (ال)**: Written as **al-** with a hyphen, retaining the spelling even before sun letters (e.g. *al-Raḥmān*, *al-Shams* in scholarly library cataloging, or assimilated *ar-Rahman* in general literature).

## 2. Reverential Honorifics & Invocations

Maintain reverent and courteous invocations after mentioning revered Islamic names:

| Figure / Entity | Arabic Invocation | Latin Symbol / Acronym | English Equivalent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Allah** | سُبْحَانَهُ وَتَعَالَى | **SWT** | *Subhanahu wa Ta'ala* ("Glory be to Him, the Exalted") |
| **Prophet Muhammad** | صَلَّى اللّٰهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ | **ﷺ** / **SAW** | *Sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam* ("May Allah bless him and grant him peace") |
| **Other Prophets & Angels** | عَلَيْهِ ٱلسَّلَامُ | **AS** | *'Alayhis-Salam* ("Peace be upon him") |
| **Male Companion (Single)** | رَضِيَ اللّٰهُ عَنْهُ | **RA** | *Radiyallahu 'anhu* ("May Allah be pleased with him") |
| **Female Companion (Single)**| رَضِيَ اللّٰهُ عَنْهَا | **RA** | *Radiyallahu 'anha* ("May Allah be pleased with her") |
| **Companions (Plural)** | رَضِيَ اللّٰهُ عَنْهُمْ | **RA** | *Radiyallahu 'anhum* ("May Allah be pleased with them") |
| **Deceased Classical Scholar**| رَحِمَهُ اللّٰهُ | **Rh.A** | *Rahimahullah* ("May Allah have mercy upon him") |

## 3. Typographical Best Practices

- Use the standardized Unicode ligature for the Prophetic blessing: `ﷺ` (`U+FDFA`).
- When writing academic articles, ensure UTF-8 encoding so macrons (*ā*, *ī*, *ū*) and underdots (*ḥ*, *ṣ*, *ḍ*, *ṭ*, *ẓ*) render legibly without encoding artifacts.
