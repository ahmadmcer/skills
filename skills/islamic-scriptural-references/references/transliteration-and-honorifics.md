# Arabic Transliteration & Islamic Honorifics Guide

Read this guide when rendering Arabic Islamic terminology into the Latin alphabet using the
ALA-LC standard, applying diacritics, and utilizing respectful honorifics.

---

## 1. ALA-LC Romanization System

The American Library Association & Library of Congress (ALA-LC) system is the academic standard
for unambiguous transliteration of Arabic into Latin script.

### Vowels & Diacritics

- **Short Vowels**: _Fatḥah_ &rarr; **a**, _Kasrah_ &rarr; **i**, _Ḍammah_ &rarr; **u**.
- **Long Vowels**:
  - Alif (ا) / Alif Maqṣūrah (ى) &rarr; **ā** (e.g. _Qur’ān_, _ṣalāh_, _Zakāh_)
  - Yā’ (ي) &rarr; **ī** (e.g. _ḥadīth_, _tafsīr_)
  - Wāw (و) &rarr; **ū** (e.g. _wuḍū’_, _ṣūm_)
- **Diphthongs**: **aw** (e.g. _tawḥīd_), **ay** (e.g. _ghayb_).
- **Tā’ Marbūṭah (ة)**: Transliterated as **h** in isolation (e.g. _Sunnah_), and as **t** in a genitive construct (_Iḍāfah_, e.g. _Sunnat al-Nabī_).
- **Definite Article (ال)**: Written as **al-** with a hyphen, retaining the spelling even before sun letters (e.g. _al-Raḥmān_, _al-Shams_ in scholarly library cataloging, or assimilated _ar-Rahman_ in general literature).

## 2. Reverential Honorifics & Invocations

Maintain reverent and courteous invocations after mentioning revered Islamic names:

| Figure / Entity                | Arabic Invocation                 | Latin Symbol / Acronym | English Equivalent                                                         |
| :----------------------------- | :-------------------------------- | :--------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Allah**                      | سُبْحَانَهُ وَتَعَالَى            | **SWT**                | _Subhanahu wa Ta'ala_ ("Glory be to Him, the Exalted")                     |
| **Prophet Muhammad**           | صَلَّى اللّٰهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ | **ﷺ** / **SAW**        | _Sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam_ ("May Allah bless him and grant him peace") |
| **Other Prophets & Angels**    | عَلَيْهِ ٱلسَّلَامُ               | **AS**                 | _'Alayhis-Salam_ ("Peace be upon him")                                     |
| **Male Companion (Single)**    | رَضِيَ اللّٰهُ عَنْهُ             | **RA**                 | _Radiyallahu 'anhu_ ("May Allah be pleased with him")                      |
| **Female Companion (Single)**  | رَضِيَ اللّٰهُ عَنْهَا            | **RA**                 | _Radiyallahu 'anha_ ("May Allah be pleased with her")                      |
| **Companions (Plural)**        | رَضِيَ اللّٰهُ عَنْهُمْ           | **RA**                 | _Radiyallahu 'anhum_ ("May Allah be pleased with them")                    |
| **Deceased Classical Scholar** | رَحِمَهُ اللّٰهُ                  | **Rh.A**               | _Rahimahullah_ ("May Allah have mercy upon him")                           |

## 3. Typographical Best Practices

- Use the standardized Unicode ligature for the Prophetic blessing: `ﷺ` (`U+FDFA`).
- When writing academic articles, ensure UTF-8 encoding so macrons (_ā_, _ī_, _ū_) and underdots (_ḥ_, _ṣ_, _ḍ_, _ṭ_, _ẓ_) render legibly without encoding artifacts.
